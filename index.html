Health IT Semester

US + Canada · 2026

A 7-week self-study course for new CS grads heading to EHR vendors, hospital integration teams or digital-health startups, in the US or Canada. Like MIT's Missing Semester, it is short and hands-on, and covers the skills school skips.

You leave with a portfolio HL7 v2 → FHIR bridge with tests, an interface spec and a runbook, plus hands-on time in four stacks.

  • No real PHI, ever. Synthetic Synthea patients, plus the broken message set in week 3.
  • One project grows every week, and every week ends with a “done when” check you can run.
  • Course vs. job: the course covers what you can practise before day one; what depends on the employer, role or jurisdiction is listed under what you'll learn on the job.

General information

Format: self-study, 7 weeks × ~10 h, plus ~4 h of prework.
Version: 1.0.0, 31 Oct 2026.
Not tested yet: the VXU stretch, broken-set case 1, SMART in a browser or a real EHR, CDS Hooks inside an EHR, Bulk FHIR with backend-services auth, the 1–2 h estimate.
Risks: needs practitioner review; rules vary by province and state.
