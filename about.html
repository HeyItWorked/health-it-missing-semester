Course vs. job

The course covers what you can practise before day one. What depends on the employer, the role or the jurisdiction is listed under what you'll learn on the job.

Using it as onboarding (for employers and mentors)

  • Swap OIE for your own integration engine, and Synthea for your test environment's synthetic patients; never production data.
  • Pair week 1 with a real shadow shift, and week 6 with your change-advisory process.
  • Review the done-when checks weekly; use the capstone rubric as the final review.
  • Replace the US vs Canada table with the rules of your own jurisdiction.
