US vs Canada: what changes

TopicUnited StatesCanada
Privacy lawHIPAA; health apps outside HIPAA: FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and state lawsPHIPA (ON), HIA (AB), FIPPA/PIPA (BC), Law 5 (QC); PIPEDA (federal, private sector)
Vendor contractBusiness associate agreement (BAA), for HIPAA-covered work onlyAgent or information-manager agreement
Core data setUSCDICACDI (CIHI)
FHIR profilesUS CoreCA Baseline; CA Core+ (still a draft)
DiagnosesICD-10-CMICD-10-CA; OHIP physician billing uses ICD-9-based codes
ProceduresICD-10-PCS (hospital); CPT (billing)CCI (hospital); provincial fee codes (billing)
DrugsRxNorm (drug concepts); NDC (product codes)CCDD (drug concepts); DIN (product codes)
Patient IDFacility MRN, often linked by an enterprise IDProvincial health card number plus facility MRN
Wider exchangeTEFCA (national network)Provincial EHRs and health information exchanges
Breach reportingHIPAA breach notification: individuals and HHSProvincial acts; under PHIPA, patients and sometimes the IPC
HostingA cloud covered by a BAA, for HIPAA workCanadian regions; check provincial rules
Also—OCAP® for First Nations data; software must be available in French in Quebec
